Privacy Policy

App: Flow Park Puzzle
Effective date: 2026-06-26
Contact: asifnasrullazade@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how Flow Park Puzzle (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles information when you use the App. We respect your privacy and collect only what is necessary to run the game, sync your progress, show advertisements, and improve gameplay.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Anonymous Account Identifier

When you launch the App, an anonymous account is automatically created on our backend (Supabase). This account is identified by a randomly generated UUID. It is not linked to your Apple ID, email address, phone number, or real name. The UUID lets us save your progress and show your nickname on the leaderboard.

1.2 Player-Provided Nickname

If you choose to set a display name, that nickname is stored on our backend and shown on the public leaderboard alongside your level and coin count. Do not include personal information in your nickname.

1.3 Gameplay Data

We store your in-game progress (current level, coins, completed levels, scores) and anonymous gameplay events (e.g. level started, level completed, rewarded ad watched) associated with your anonymous account, in order to sync progress across sessions and improve the game.

1.4 Advertising Identifiers

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. If you grant App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission, AdMob may access your device’s Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) to deliver personalized ads and measure performance. If you decline, only contextual (non-personalized) ads are shown. You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

1.5 In-App Purchases

In-app purchases (such as remove_ads and coin packs) are processed by Apple via StoreKit. We do not receive your payment details. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can unlock the corresponding content.

1.6 Information We Do Not Collect

  • Real name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Precise or coarse location
  • Photos, contacts, microphone, or camera data
  • Health or financial data
  • Browsing or search history outside the App

2. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Supabase — backend storage for the anonymous account, leaderboard, and analytics events. See supabase.com/privacy.
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit — in-app purchase processing. See apple.com/legal/privacy.

3. How We Use Data

  • To save and sync your game progress across sessions and devices.
  • To display the public leaderboard.
  • To deliver and measure advertisements.
  • To analyze gameplay and improve features.
  • To process in-app purchases.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share data with third parties except the service providers listed in Section 2, which act on our behalf or as independent controllers for the limited purposes described above.

5. Data Retention

Anonymous account data is retained for as long as the App is installed on your device or until you request deletion. Analytics event records are retained for up to 24 months.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads: deny the ATT prompt or revoke it in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Delete your data: email asifnasrullazade@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data” and we will remove your anonymous account, nickname, and progress within 30 days.
  • Reset local data: deleting the App from your device removes all locally cached data.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Users

Your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, including the United States and the European Union. We rely on the standard contractual safeguards offered by our service providers.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the App or on this page. The “Effective date” above shows when the current version was published.
