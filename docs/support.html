Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Swipe cars up, down, left, or right to slide them along the lot. Free the blocked car so it can reach the exit. Trucks and buses only move along their length. Solve the puzzle in as few moves as possible to earn more coins.

I lost my progress after reinstalling the app.

Your progress is automatically synced to our cloud backend. If it isn't restored after reinstall, please email us with your player nickname and approximate current level, and we'll help recover it.

I bought "Remove Ads" but ads are still showing.

Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID that made the purchase. If ads still appear, force-close and reopen the app. Still stuck? Email us with your Apple ID's email and the approximate purchase date.

My coins didn't arrive after purchase.

Consumable coin packs are credited within a few seconds. If not, open the Shop and pull down to refresh, or tap Restore Purchases. Contact us if the issue persists — App Store receipts guarantee we can credit you.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at asifnasrullazade@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your player nickname. We'll remove your anonymous account, progress, and leaderboard entry within 30 days.

Is the app free?

Yes. The app is free to play with occasional advertisements. You can optionally purchase coin packs or the one-time "Remove Ads" upgrade. There are no subscriptions.

What ages is the app suitable for?

Rated 4+. No violence, no chat, no user-generated content. Ads may appear.
